# Overview

The landing page presents Poryadok.Shifts as a calm operational product for businesses with shift-based teams. It prioritizes real product evidence, a clear workflow, and an honest pilot offer over generic marketing claims.

# Colors

Use cool neutral surfaces with one cobalt accent. Blue is reserved for primary actions, links, focus states, and small product accents. Success uses green only when it communicates a confirmed benefit. Dark mode stays in the same cool neutral family.

# Typography

Inter provides a practical business tone and full Cyrillic support. The hero uses a large, compact headline. Section headings are clearly smaller. Body copy remains readable at 14 to 18 pixels with generous line height. Letter spacing is never negative.

# Layout

The maximum content width is 1200 pixels. The hero is asymmetric, with product copy on the left and real interface screenshots on the right. Sections use open layouts, horizontal rules, and alternating editorial compositions instead of repeated equal cards.

# Elevation & Depth

Depth is reserved for real product screenshots and interactive navigation layers. Ordinary content sections use borders rather than heavy shadows. Gradients, glow, glass decoration, and ornamental blobs are not used.

# Shapes

Controls and surfaces use restrained 8 pixel radii. Circular shapes are limited to workflow markers. The mobile product frame uses a larger radius because it represents a physical device.

# Components

Primary buttons use a solid cobalt fill with white text. Secondary actions use text or a quiet bordered surface. Product screenshots are always real application states. Capability rows use Phosphor icons and horizontal dividers instead of feature cards.

# Do's and Don'ts

Do show real product screenshots, concise operational copy, distinct role outcomes, and clear payment terminology. Do preserve accessible focus states and reduced motion support.

Do not invent analytics, prices, customer logos, or testimonials. Do not use gradients, purple, glowing decoration, nested cards, or generic AI product imagery.
